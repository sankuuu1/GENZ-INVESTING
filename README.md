# Groww First100: Gen Z Onboarding & 100-Day Investing Framework
> **Product Growth Case Study & Interactive Prototype**  
> Designed for Groww (Growth & Product Internship Thesis)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Groww First100** is an institutional-grade, SEBI-compliant growth framework designed to transition Gen Z first-time investors from speculative impulses into disciplined, long-term wealth creators. 

Rather than relying on gamified FOMO or unregulated social signals, First100 introduces structured guardrails, a mandatory 3-question capital & horizon onboarding check, and a phased 100-day curriculum with embedded risk cooling-off mechanisms.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Zero-Friction Guided Onboarding
- **Identity & Capital Personalization:** Collects investor name, risk appetite, and initial monthly SIP target (`₹500`, `₹1,000`, `₹2,500+`).
- **Goal-Linked Mandate:** Directs users into broad-market index funds (Nifty 50) and low-volatility ETFs before unlocking single equities or derivatives.

### 2. Institutional Guardrail Engine
- **FOMO Volatility Pause:** Circuit-breaker pop-up when high-beta equities surge >10% in a single trading session, emphasizing risk-adjusted valuation over momentum.
- **F&O Derivative Cooling-Off:** Mandates SEBI regulatory disclosures (noting 9 out of 10 individual traders incur net losses in equity F&O) prior to derivative access.
- **Dip Opportunity Calculator:** Systematic SIP top-up simulator for market corrections without emotional distress.

### 3. The 100-Day Milestone Tracker
- **Stage 1 (Days 1–30):** Habit formation & rupee-cost averaging via automated Nifty 50 SIP.
- **Stage 2 (Days 31–60):** Asset allocation fundamentals (Debt vs. Equity vs. Gold sovereign assets).
- **Stage 3 (Days 61–90):** Reading balance sheets, quarterly statements, and ROCE metrics.
- **Stage 4 (Days 91–100):** Graduation to self-directed diversified investing.
